Privacy Policy

Reel Empire: Idle Tycoon · Rushing Technologies · Last updated November 13, 2026

Short version: Reel Empire doesn't need an account, doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across apps and doesn't sell any data. It sends anonymous play statistics, which you can turn off in Settings.

Your saved game

Your progress (your harbours, crew, species book and settings) is saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud storage so it can continue on your other devices. iCloud is Apple's service; we don't receive or see your save.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the game an anonymous identifier for your Apple Account and, if you share it, your first name (used only as your harbour master's name, which you can change in Settings). We never receive your email address. The identifier is used only to tie your purchases to your account so they follow you to a new device. You can sign out, or delete everything, in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them. For this, RevenueCat receives an app user ID (a random identifier made on your device, or the Sign in with Apple identifier if you signed in) and your purchase history (which products you bought and when). It's used only to deliver and restore what you bought. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. The game also records on your device which purchases it has delivered so nothing is granted twice.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends Apple your scores (an empire score, species caught, harbour stars, your heaviest fish and your best derby) and the achievements you finish, to show on Game Center's leaderboards and your Game Center profile under your Game Center name. This goes to Apple only, under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive it. You can sign out of Game Center in the iPhone's Settings.

The weekend derby

The derby's table is made on your device. The other skippers on it are invented by the game; they are not other players, and your score is not sent to us.

Crew chests

Crew chests are free on a timer and can also be opened with gems, which are earned by playing and can be bought. The chance of each rarity is shown in the game before you open one.

Anonymous play statistics

To see where players get stuck, the game sends small anonymous events to our own server (run on Cloudflare): for example that a goal was reached, a star gained or a harbour opened. Each event also says the game version, the device model and system version, the language setting, and a frame-rate sample. Events carry a random identifier made on your device, which is hashed on your device before it is sent. They aren't linked to your name, Apple Account, purchases or advertising identifier, we don't store your IP address, and events older than a year are deleted. You can turn this off in Settings > Anonymous play statistics.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders (your docks are full, a boat is back, a chest is ready) are scheduled on your device itself. Nothing is sent to a server for them. You can turn them off in Settings.

What we don't do

No ads. No advertising identifier. No tracking across apps or websites. No sale of data. No contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from children. Purchases can be restricted with Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete account and start over erases your harbours from the device and from your iCloud, signs you out, and gives the statistics a new anonymous identifier. To ask about anything else, use the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
