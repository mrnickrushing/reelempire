Need help? Report a problem or ask a question on our support board. Include your device model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Restoring purchases

Open SHOP in the game, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases. One-time purchases (the Deckhand's Pack, Captain's Ledger, Second Boat Out and Night Crew) come back on any device signed in with the same Apple Account. Gems travel with your iCloud save; a restore brings back what is kept for good, not gems. A Season Pass belongs to the season it was bought in.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your saved game

Your harbours are saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud so they follow you to a new iPhone or iPad. MORE > Delete account and start over deletes both.

Your account

Signing in with Apple is optional. MORE > Delete account and start over erases everything and signs you out.

No ads

Reel Empire has no ads. Everything in the game can be earned by playing.
