Reel Empire: Idle Tycoon

A fishing harbour tycoon for iPhone and iPad by Rushing Technologies

Start with one rowboat on a quiet lake. Catch fish, haul them up the dock, clean and pack them, and sell them at the market. Hire skippers, sign on a crew, fill the species book and grow your harbour across six waters, from the lake to the Arctic. No forced ads: an advert only plays when you ask for one.
